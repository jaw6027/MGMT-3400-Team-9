              ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLES · USD · NOT PERSONAL QUOTES

              Electricity-use charges only; fees and installation are excluded. Here is how three different homes might compare. These are examples, not usage thresholds or recommended system sizes for your home. Opening them does not change your bills or solar settings.

              Assumptions behind these examples

              Electricity price: $0.18/kWh · Sunlight: 4.5 peak sun hours/day · System performance: 80% · Efficiency improvements: none assumed.

              Simplified assumption: every unit of solar generated offsets electricity worth the full $0.18 retail price. This may require matching daytime use or retail-value export credits; neither is guaranteed. These examples do not model hourly use or seasonal changes. Actual export credits may be lower.

              Electricity charges only: fixed utility fees, maintenance, monitoring, installation, financing, batteries, and replacement costs are excluded. Monthly figures average a full year and are rounded; generation varies by season.

              To explore your own situation, enter your bills and review the solar assumptions with a qualified provider. Solar produces electricity; efficiency improvements reduce the amount your home uses.

              Clear assumptions. Better conversations.

              This planning tool uses USD and annual averages. Solar output = system size × peak sun hours × 365 × performance. Solar used at home is capped at demand after efficiency improvements; the remainder is exported. Savings compare variable electricity costs before and after upgrades, less maintenance and monitoring fees. Payback = net upfront cost ÷ positive annual savings.

              Illustrative estimates are not a quote or a guarantee. The solar scenario excludes fixed utility charges; the bill-based comparison retains inferred remaining charges. Both estimates exclude batteries, financing, panel degradation, equipment replacement, rate changes, and seasonal or hourly matching. No live meters or smart devices are connected. Nothing is sent to a server.               Quick answers

              How do I review my electricity bills?

              Enter January–December usage in kWh and bill costs in USD. All 12 months are needed for your energy review. Zero is allowed. Use bills from the same calendar year.

              Can I use a photo of my bill?

              Choose a PNG, JPG, or WebP bill photo in Homeowner Energy Review. The full app reads it locally in your browser. Review and correct all three fields before adding them. Unreadable values are flagged, and existing months require replacement confirmation.

              Are the solar savings guaranteed?

              No. Results depend on the rates, solar output, fees, and other assumptions shown in Current costs vs. solar. Confirm these with your utility and installer before investing.

              How do I request a home assessment?

              Use Request an inspection visit in the home questionnaire even if you do not know the answers or have all your bills. After a complete bill review, Request a Home Assessment also appears in your personalized recommendation. It creates a request file for you to share with a provider. This demo does not send it or book an appointment.

              Is my information saved?

              Entries are cleared on reload unless you choose Save progress. That saves monthly numbers, solar settings, and example-data labels on this browser. Home questionnaire answers are included only if you select the separate save option. Delete saved progress removes that saved copy. Bill photos, full recognized text, and personal contact details are never included or uploaded. Any report or assessment file you choose to download remains your responsibility to share or delete.
