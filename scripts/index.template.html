      Assumptions used

      Estimated annual cost = grid electricity + remaining bill charges − export credits + maintenance + monitoring. Remaining charges are inferred from your bills, not a verified utility fee breakdown, and are held constant. Confirm taxes, minimum bills, demand charges, and export rules with your utility.
