This planning tool uses USD and annual averages. Solar output = system size × peak sun hours × 365 × performance. Solar used at home is capped at demand after efficiency improvements; the remainder is exported. Savings compare variable electricity costs before and after upgrades, less maintenance and monitoring fees. Payback = net upfront cost ÷ positive annual savings.

Illustrative estimates are not a quote or a guarantee. Results exclude fixed utility charges, batteries, financing, panel degradation, equipment replacement, rate changes, and seasonal or hourly matching. No live meters or smart devices are connected.
