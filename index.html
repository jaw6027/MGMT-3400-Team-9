        Quick answers

        How do I review my electricity bills?

        Enter January–December usage in kWh and bill costs in USD. All 12 months are needed for your energy review. Zero is allowed. Use bills from the same calendar year.

        Can I use a photo of my bill?

        Choose a PNG, JPG, or WebP bill photo in Homeowner Energy Review. The full app reads it locally in your browser. Review and correct all three fields before adding them. Unreadable values are flagged, and existing months require replacement confirmation.

        Are the solar savings guaranteed?

        No. Results depend on the rates, solar output, fees, and other assumptions shown in Current costs vs. solar. Confirm these with your utility and installer before investing.

        How do I request a home assessment?

        Complete your bill review and use Request a Home Assessment in your personalized recommendation. It creates a request file for you to share with a provider. This demo does not send it or book an appointment.

        Is my information saved?

        Entries are cleared on reload unless you choose Save progress. That saves only monthly numbers, solar settings, and example-data labels on this browser. Delete saved progress removes that saved copy. Bill photos, full recognized text, and personal contact details are never included or uploaded. Any report or assessment file you choose to download remains your responsibility to share or delete.
