    A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

    Good questions.
    Clear answers.

    Here are a few things to know
    before we connect.

    Is the consultation really free?

    Yes. Our business concept offers free initial consultations by phone or in person, so homeowners can ask questions before considering their next step.

    Do you install the solar panels?

    No. Kern Solar Connect is a consultation and referral concept. Solar installers would assess your home, provide quotes, and handle installation. No installer partnerships have been established for this class project.

    How much could I save with solar?

    There’s no single answer for every home. Potential savings depend on your energy use, system costs, roof conditions, utility rates, and other factors. An installer would need to assess your home and provide an estimate. Savings are not guaranteed.

    What should I bring to a consultation?

    Have a recent electricity bill, ideally 12 months of usage, home basics, appliance information, and your solar questions ready. Our required energy worksheet can help. Use fictional details for this class demo.

    Can I book a real appointment here?

    This website is a MGMT 3400 student project. The form demonstrates the consultation request process; it does not send a request or book a real appointment.
